# Navigation — November 2026

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-11-23 · [Roadmap X1 / D4](../plans/roadmap-2026-11.md#6-owner-decisions) · [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-shop-navigation.md)

For the [elder shopkeeper](../PRD.md#24-users) on a cheap Android phone: fewer places to look, familiar Vietnamese labels, explicit confirmation. D4 authorizes this agent-written decision; X2–X6 proceed without another approval. This proposal supersedes the navigation and tap targets in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md), not the shipped UI; each implementation updates its boards first.

## Final structure

| Tab / Lucide icon           | Contents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Kho** / `Package`         | `/inventory`: **Sản phẩm** (sellable inventory grid) and **Tồn kho** (stock list with all/low/out/in-stock filters), switched in place. One **Nhập hàng** button above both segments opens `/receipts`. Product details retain price, units, adjustments and movements.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Báo cáo** / `ChartColumn` | `/dashboard`: first, one daily row **Chi · Chốt sổ · Công nợ**, each one tap away; then daily totals, **top-products** (D3), chart, alerts and digest. Recent sales and transaction CSV/XLSX export stay below reports. Recognition report and price-list export have no app entry or route (D3).                                                                                                                  |
| **Tài khoản** / `User`      | `/account`: three groups below identity: **Cửa hàng** (store picker, members/invites, bank, stock threshold); **Dễ sử dụng** (elder mode, spoken total, text size, VI/EN); **Nâng cao**, collapsed (PIN set/change, device sessions/switching/add person, voice bubble permission/on/off, tile setup, voice donation opt-in). Sign-out stays visible below groups. Preserve existing permissions and every option. |

Camera (`Focus`, “Mở camera”) stays bottom-left on the shop bar; camera footer links back to Kho. Remove the floating shop assistant: **Trợ lý** (`MessageCircleText`) lives in the Báo cáo header. Replace the camera footer chat circle with **Nói** (`Mic`) → `/voice`; keep the header assistant's long-press shortcut and voice deep links, remove the duplicate `voice_sale` chip. The optional Android voice bubble remains a system surface, configured under Nâng cao; do not add another in-app bubble. Assistant feature flags still apply; voice's own button must remain usable without the assistant flag.

**Nhập hàng** is one `/receipts` flow: history/drafts → take receipt photo → editable review with photo, quantity, cost, mapping, total, payment source and stock effect together → **Xác nhận nhập kho**. A history row opens the same review; confirmed/discarded receipts are read-only. Remove the separate “Xong, xem lại” stage and the Dashboard receipt chip. Camera document shutter/chip, digest drafts and stock restock CTA enter this same flow; remove the parallel “Nhập trong trợ lý” entry (review can still ask the assistant for help). Confirmation creates one purchase expense and stock movement, then returns to Kho/Tồn kho with a success notice; no second stock-intake screen. Manual product adjustments and the expense-only Nhập category keep their existing meaning.

**Sale (X5):** with an empty cart and exactly one confirmed scan item, `+` adds it and opens Pay directly with **Tiền mặt** selected; QR is beside Cash and nothing is recorded yet. Existing carts, multiple/uncertain matches and corrections keep Cart, now with visible **Tiền mặt** and **QR** actions that preselect Pay's method; retain credit, units, quantity edits, tender/change and offline idempotency. A direct `/pay` with no method keeps today's transfer default, including voice transfer; an empty cart never produces a sale.

**Elder mode (X6/D5):** ask “Dùng giao diện dễ nhìn?” once in create/join onboarding, default **Bật**, before membership completion; persist the answer on this device with a completion marker, retain existing users' choices and do not ask on every store switch. Keep its switch under Tài khoản/Dễ sử dụng. Today `Home.tsx` changes only the launch destination; size support already exists in `fontScale.ts`, ProductTile, CartPill, CheckButton and QrPill, and enabling elder mode enables spoken totals. Extend that support to the new tabs, segments, daily row and receipt/payment controls: visible Vietnamese icon labels, ≥125% text floor, 80 dp primary sell/pay targets, spoken total/change (independently switchable). Start on Kho/Sản phẩm once per launch, with camera unmounted until opened; OFF retains camera-first. Both modes use the same routes and actions.

**Links/back contract:** keep old alias files for installed clients/notifications; internal callers use canonical URLs. `/stock` (including old low-stock links) replaces to Kho/Tồn kho; carry supported filter parameters, use `status=all` when absent, as today. Segment taps change local/URL state without pushing; Back never alternates stock/inventory. `/receipt/:id` replaces into the unified receipt review; missing/deleted ID shows a message and **Về nhập hàng**, never a blank screen. Keep all other path/query contracts (assistant entry/prompt/receiptId, enrollment context, join token). Cold detail links Back to their owning tab (products/receipts → Kho; sales/debts/digest → Báo cáo; payment/cart → camera); aliases add no Back step. Retain authentication, membership, PIN and role gates; an unavailable action explains why and offers the owning tab.

**External entries:** today's native notification observer pushes `data.url`; digest producer sends `/digest?entry=push` plus `business_date`. Keep that payload and cold/warm handling. Low-stock alerts today are the shop banner/Dashboard alert, not a stock push producer; update them to `/inventory?segment=stock&status=low`, while accepting old `/stock`. Keep `taphoa://voice?entry=bubble&returnToPrevious=1`, `taphoa://voice?entry=tile` and `entry=shortcut` unchanged: Confirm/Close from the bubble returns to the previous app; tile/shortcut dismiss within the app. Elder launch must not intercept any external destination.

## Tap budget (before → target)

Count successful UI taps, not waits, swipes, setup/permissions or corrections; state the starting surface explicitly. Camera is ready, scan is one certain item and cart empty; cash example is 15,000đ with a 100,000đ note, QR assumes a configured bank. Expense/close examples enter 5,000đ using `5`, `000`; use the same start before/after.

| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash sale / after shutter                               | `+` → cart pill → QR → Tiền mặt → 100.000 → Đã nhận tiền = **6** (**5** exact cash) | `+` → Pay cash → 100.000 → Đã nhận tiền = **3** (**2** exact cash); Pay opening costs no tap, ≤4 gate.                        |

## Risks / open points

- Validate 320 dp/200% labels and 80 dp payment controls on an inexpensive Android phone; do not shrink targets to fit the bar.
- OCR correction time is unbounded; keep photo, quantities, mapping and costs visible before the single confirmation, with retry preserving edits.
- Explicit capture inside Nhập hàng must work even when passive document detection misses; X3's photo test must cover that path.
- Test cold/warm push, legacy aliases and bubble return on a device; emulator/web alone cannot verify system surfaces.
