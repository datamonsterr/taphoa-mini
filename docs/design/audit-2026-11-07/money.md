## Differences and proposed failing tests

Each count below is one independently actionable difference. Fixture text such as product names and amounts is not itself a gap.

- **D1 — major, Dashboard:** the board goes from chart to three compact alert pills ([board](../canvas/Dashboard.dc.html#L65)); the app inserts a full “Sắp hết hàng” section with item rows and “Xem tất cả” ([DashboardScreen](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/DashboardScreen.tsx#L139), [AlertsSection](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/AlertsSection.tsx#L174)). The app also places “Đơn gần đây” and “Xuất giao dịch” after Chốt sổ, neither present on the board ([DashboardScreen](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/DashboardScreen.tsx#L157)). **Jest:** `dashboard_matches_board_without_extra_sections` — with low-stock data, assert `queryByText('Sắp hết hàng')`, `queryByText('Đơn gần đây')` and `queryByText('Xuất giao dịch')` are null.
- **D2 — major, Receipt:** the board subtitle contains supplier, date, and lowercase status ([board](../canvas/Receipt.dc.html#L23)); the app prints only date and status ([ReceiptReviewScreen](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/receipts/ReceiptReviewScreen.tsx#L296)). **RNTL:** `summary_includes_supplier_in_subtitle` — for supplier Minh Phát and date 27/10 assert text `Minh Phát · 27/10 · bản nháp`.
- **D3 — major, ReceiptEdit:** the board prefixes the read/fix counts with supplier ([board](../canvas/ReceiptEdit.dc.html#L25)); the app only renders counts ([ReceiptReviewScreen](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/receipts/ReceiptReviewScreen.tsx#L296)). **RNTL:** `review_subtitle_includes_supplier` — assert `Minh Phát · 4 dòng đọc được · 1 cần xem` for that fixture.
- **D4 — major, ReceiptEdit:** board photo strip says `Ảnh hóa đơn · 12 dòng chữ` ([board](../canvas/ReceiptEdit.dc.html#L30)); the app renders only `Ảnh hóa đơn` ([ReceiptReviewScreen](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/receipts/ReceiptReviewScreen.tsx#L340), [strings](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/receipts/strings.ts#L30)). **RNTL:** `review_photo_shows_ocr_count` — for 12 detected text lines assert that exact text.
- **D5 — major, ScanDoc:** both board action chips have leading receipt/chat icons ([board](../canvas/ScanDoc.dc.html#L65)); captured app chips are text-only, confirmed by [NextStepChip](../../../apps/mobile/src/components/assistant/NextStepChip.tsx#L24). **RNTL:** `receipt_actions_show_leading_icons` — assert receipt and assistant icon testIDs are present in their respective chip testIDs.
- **D6 — major, Debts:** for `Chị Lan`, the board shows avatar `L`, while the app shows `C` ([board](../canvas/Debts.dc.html#L39), [customerRow](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/debts/customerRow.ts#L21)). **RNTL:** `honorific_customer_avatar_uses_given_name` — assert the Chị Lan row contains `L`.
- **D7 — minor, Customer:** board centers the overdue badge under the balance ([board](../canvas/Customer.dc.html#L28)); app's badge aligns to the left edge of the card ([OverdueBadge](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/debts/OverdueBadge.tsx#L31)). **RNTL:** `customer_overdue_badge_is_centered` — assert the customer badge style has `alignSelf: 'center'`.
- **D8 — major, Digest:** board restock rows have product photo, `Còn 3 · bán 6/ngày`, and `2 thùng` chip ([board](../canvas/Digest.dc.html#L38)); app renders name, `× quantity`, and optional cost with no photo or stock/sales-rate detail ([DigestScreen](../../../apps/mobile/src/features/digest/DigestScreen.tsx#L205)). The `digest:populated` fixture produces the observed name, quantity and cost row. **RNTL:** `restock_row_shows_photo_stock_rate_and_unit` — with populated Hảo Hảo data assert photo testID, `Còn 3 · bán 6/ngày`, and `2 thùng` are visible.

**Counts: 7 major, 1 minor.** D1 groups extra dashboard sections as one layout difference.
