## Findings

1. **Major — transfer total is clipped.** In the populated QR capture the lower portion of `10.000đ` is covered/clipped immediately above the QR card; the board shows the entire total (`docs/design/canvas/Pay.dc.html:30`). The amount is rendered at `apps/mobile/src/features/pay/PayScreen.tsx:321` within the vertically constrained screen (`:667`, QR card `:679`, order strip `:755`). The photo and full line price are visible in this one-item capture. **Proposed Playwright test `pay total remains fully visible with a populated cart`:** at 390×844 with the seeded product and transfer selected, assert the total's rendered bounding rectangle has full line height and its bottom is no greater than the QR card's top; use stable test IDs if necessary.

2. **Major — customer row differs in initial, phone and activity text.** For `Chị Lan`, picker renders `C` and `0912345678`, while the board shows `L` and `0912 345 678` (`docs/design/canvas/CustomerPick.dc.html:40`); selected credit row likewise shows the unformatted phone (`PayCredit.dc.html:62`, `apps/mobile/src/features/pay/PayScreen.tsx:344`). Picker takes the first character (`apps/mobile/src/features/debts/customerRow.ts:22`, `CustomerPicker.tsx:165`) and prints the raw phone (`:174`). Board also shows `tuần trước` for a settled customer (`CustomerPick.dc.html:42`); app deliberately renders age only for debt (`CustomerPicker.tsx:200`). The #184 closure explains that the API lacks last-activity data, so this part requires a data/design decision rather than treating it as a regression. **Proposed RNTL test `customer picker displays the board identity`:** render settled Chị Lan with phone `0912345678` and assert `L`, `0912 345 678`, and, with a supplied last-activity fixture, `tuần trước` are visible. Current code fails these assertions.

3. **Major — sale-detail void explanation and placeholder differ.** Board says `Hủy toàn bộ đơn. Hoàn lại khoản đã thu và điều chỉnh báo cáo, tồn kho, sổ nợ.` and the empty reason field says `Khách trả hàng` (`docs/design/canvas/SaleDetail.dc.html:5`). App instead says `Hủy toàn bộ đơn và hoàn lại khoản đã thu cho khách. Báo cáo, tồn kho và sổ nợ sẽ được điều chỉnh. Thao tác được ghi lại cùng lý do.` (`apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/transactionStrings.ts:25`) and supplies no placeholder (`apps/mobile/src/features/sales/SaleDetailScreen.tsx:103`). **Proposed RNTL test `sale detail uses board void copy`:** assert the exact board explanation and `getByPlaceholderText('Khách trả hàng')` are present for a completed Lead sale.
