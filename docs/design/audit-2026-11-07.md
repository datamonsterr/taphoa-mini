## Gaps

### Sign-in header is 64 px too high

**Severity: minor.** The static board places its 80 px logo at y=80 (`canvas/SignIn.dc.html:18`); the signed-out web screenshot at the same 390×844 viewport places it at y=16. The title, subtitle and form consequently also start 64 px higher. The board's populated email and switch-person card represent a different account state and are not included in this finding. The shared `Frame` uses `space[4] + insets.top` at `apps/mobile/src/features/onboarding/kit.tsx:75`; sign-in selects its branded variant at `apps/mobile/src/features/auth/SignInScreen.tsx:155`. Adjust sign-in's top spacing without forcing the same padding on unrelated flows.

**Proposed Playwright acceptance test:** at 390×844 with zero web safe-area inset, open `/sign-in`, locate the branded logo (add a stable test ID), and assert its bounding box `y` is 80 px. Today it is 16 px, so the test fails. Also assert the form remains usable at a shorter viewport.

The stock board shows `bán 6/ngày` while the app only renders a threshold, but the closure of #234 explicitly records that daily sales rate is unavailable and this omission was intentional. Do not file that difference as a new regression without revisiting that decision.
